Daily Routine

In this lesson you will learn how to describe your daily routine in Brazilian Portuguese. You will practice common verbs, useful expressions and everyday conversations about your morning, afternoon and evening activities.

💡 Useful Expressions

These expressions are useful when talking about your daily routine.

Eu acordo às sete horas. (I wake up at seven o'clock.)

Eu tomo café da manhã. (I have breakfast.)

Eu vou para o trabalho. (I go to work.)

Eu estudo português. (I study Portuguese.)

Eu durmo às dez horas. (I go to sleep at ten o'clock.)

🗣️ Daily Routine Dialogue

Lucas: Que horas você acorda?
(What time do you wake up?)

Ana: Eu acordo às sete horas.
(I wake up at seven o'clock.)

Lucas: O que você faz depois?
(What do you do afterwards?)

Ana: Eu tomo café da manhã e vou trabalhar.
(I have breakfast and go to work.)

Lucas: Você estuda à noite?
(Do you study at night?)

Ana: Sim! Eu estudo português antes de dormir.
(Yes! I study Portuguese before sleeping.)

🇧🇷 Brazilian Culture

Many Brazilians start their day early. Breakfast is usually light, and lunch is considered the main meal of the day. It's also common to ask friends about their daily routine when getting to know them.

Practice

Exercise 1

Practice describing your daily routine to a classmate. Use expressions like: Eu acordo às sete horas. and Eu vou para o trabalho.

Exercise 2

Write five sentences using these verbs: acordar, estudar, almoçar, trabalhar and dormir.

Exercise 3

Write a short paragraph describing your complete daily routine from morning to night.
