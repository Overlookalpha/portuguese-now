Weather

In this lesson you will learn how to talk about the weather in Brazilian Portuguese. You will learn useful vocabulary, common expressions and everyday conversations about sunny, rainy, cloudy and windy days.

🌤️ Weather Vocabulary

English Portuguese Audio
Weather Tempo
Sun Sol
Rain Chuva
Cloud Nuvem
Wind Vento
Hot Quente
Cold Frio
Cloudy Nublado
Sunny Ensolarado
Storm Tempestade

💡 Useful Expressions

These expressions are very common when talking about the weather.

Como está o tempo? (How is the weather?)

Está quente. (It's hot.)

Está frio. (It's cold.)

Está chovendo. (It's raining.)

Hoje está ensolarado. (Today it's sunny.)

🌦️ Weather Dialogue

Lucas: Como está o tempo hoje?
(How is the weather today?)

Ana: Está ensolarado e muito quente.
(It is sunny and very hot.)

Lucas: Vai chover mais tarde?
(Is it going to rain later?)

Ana: Acho que não.
(I don't think so.)

Lucas: Então vamos ao parque!
(Then let's go to the park!)

Ana: Ótima ideia!
(Great idea!)

🇧🇷 Brazilian Culture

Brazil has many different climates. While cities in the south can have cold winters, the north is usually hot and humid all year. Brazilians often begin conversations by commenting on the weather, especially during very hot days or heavy rain.

🎧 Listening Practice

Repeat these expressions aloud:

  • Como está o tempo?
  • Está quente.
  • Está frio.
  • Está chovendo.
  • Hoje está ensolarado.

Practice

Exercise 1

Practice asking and answering questions about the weather with a friend. Use expressions like: Como está o tempo? and Está ensolarado.

Exercise 2

Write five sentences using these words: sol, chuva, vento, frio and quente.

Exercise 3

Write a short dialogue between two people talking about today's weather and deciding what they will do.
